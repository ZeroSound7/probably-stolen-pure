[h1]可能是纯的 / Probably Pure[/h1]
把含杂质溶液转化为纯溶液。

[h2]这个 Mod 加什么[/h2]
[b]纯溶液净化机[/b]：一台 2×3 的机器，放进含杂质的试剂和一张「工艺芯片」，过夜把杂质转化为目标纯溶液。同一台机器换芯片就能处理不同液体；芯片可重复使用，不会消耗。
[b]5 张工艺芯片[/b]：酸 / 碱 / 脂合剂 / N-羟基甲碱（保留稳定剂）/ 还原剂。

[h2]怎么用[/h2]
[list]
[*]在杰克逊博士（发明家 / Dr. Jackson）的货单里购买：机器 300，芯片各 100。机器已拥有后按 25% 概率补货；芯片在未拥有时上架。
[*]把机器摆在店里，接上电源，放入装有液体的容器 + 对应工艺芯片，睡觉过夜。
[*]质量决定纯度上限：Q=0 → 98%，Q=25 → 99%，Q=50 → 100%。
[*]性能决定每晚去除量：P=0 → 10 mL，P=50 → 20 mL；效率降低耗电（基础耗电 8）。
[*]放错芯片或液体里没有目标物时不会工作、不耗电，芯片也不会被消耗。
[*]N-羟基甲碱配方里的稳定剂会被保留，不会被当成杂质去掉。
[/list]

[h2]⚠ 第一次启用：先启用，重启，再读档[/h2]
[list]
[*]订阅后在「模组」菜单点「审核并启用」，然后[b]必须重启游戏[/b]，再载入存档。
[*]如果 Mod 还没实际加载（模组列表里显示警告/未加载）就载入了含本 Mod 物品的存档，游戏会把未知物品换成占位物，可能导致这次读档异常、物品栏显示为空。这不是存档损坏：不要保存，退出游戏、确认 Mod 已启用，重进再读档即可。
[*]第一次启用前建议备份存档（%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Questing Goose Studio\Probably Stolen\）。
[/list]

[h2]English[/h2]
[b]Probably Pure[/b] turns impure solutions into pure ones.

[list]
[*]Adds a 2x3 [b]Solution Purifier[/b] and 5 reusable [b]process chips[/b]: acid, base, lipobond agent, N-methylol base (the stabilizer is preserved) and reducing agent.
[*]Load a liquid container and the matching chip, then sleep. The impurity is converted into the target liquid; total volume stays the same. Chips are never consumed.
[*]Quality sets the purity cap (Q=0 → 98%, Q=25 → 99%, Q=50 → 100%); performance sets the nightly removal (P=0 → 10 mL, P=50 → 20 mL); efficiency lowers the power draw (base 8).
[*]Wrong chip or no target liquid in the mixture: the machine does nothing, draws no power, and the chip is not consumed.
[*]Sold by Dr. Jackson (the inventor). Machine 300, chips 100 each. Once the machine is owned it restocks with a 25% chance; chips are listed until owned.
[/list]

[h2]⚠ First enable: activate, restart, then load[/h2]
[list]
[*]Subscribe, click “Review and enable” in the Mods menu, [b]restart the game[/b], and only then load your save.
[*]If you load a save containing this mod’s items while the mod is not actually running (warning/unloaded in the mod list), the game replaces unknown items with placeholders: that load may fail or the inventory may appear empty. Your save is not corrupted—do not save, quit, make sure the mod is enabled, restart and load again.
[*]Back up your saves before the first enable: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Questing Goose Studio\Probably Stolen\.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Game version: 0.46D. No prerequisites. Vanilla machines, recipes and save format are untouched.
[*]After subscribing, enable it once in the in-game “Mods” menu and restart the game.
[*]Before uninstalling or disabling, sell/destroy any purifiers and chips already in your save to avoid unknown items.
[*]Not tested together with mods that also modify shops, liquids or Dr. Jackson's stock.
[/list]
